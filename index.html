ABOUT EDU-PREDICT

Turning Academic Records into Career Insight

EDU-PREDICT is an institutional big data intelligence system designed for universities and engineering colleges to connect academic achievement with real-world industry employability.

Academic records provide valuable insight into a student's learning journey. Grades, attendance, projects, certifications, internships, and demonstrated skills can help institutions understand student progress and career preparation.

EDU-PREDICT organizes these records into meaningful insights, helping placement officers review student profiles, understand employability indicators, and make better-informed decisions about placement preparation.

Our Institutional Mission

To equip higher education institutions with transparent, evidence-based predictive intelligence. By analyzing multi-dimensional student performance indicators across academic records, practical projects, certifications, and technical skills, EDU-PREDICT enables educators to identify skill gaps early and coordinate timely developmental interventions before campus recruitment begins.

🔭

Our Strategic Vision

To transform college placement preparation from an end-of-year reactive rush into a continuous, structured, and verifiable student growth journey. We envision an educational ecosystem where every graduate enters the job market equipped with market-aligned competencies and a clear career pathway.

⚠️

Addressing the Higher Education-to-Employability Gap

Across higher education, more than 68% of engineering students require dedicated placement readiness interventions. While traditional transcripts reflect academic diligence through CGPA and classroom attendance, industry corporate recruiters evaluate applied technical stacks, database architecture, problem-solving, and collaborative professional competencies.

EDU-PREDICT bridges this critical divide by aggregating classroom achievement with practical experience indicators—providing placement officers with real-time insight into each student's career readiness profile.

TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE

Big Data & Machine Learning Engine

Built from the ground up on distributed big data infrastructure to process massive institutional student cohorts with zero performance bottlenecks.

Distributed Storage

Apache Hadoop HDFS

Fault-tolerant, multi-node distributed file system storing millions of historical and live student records with block-level replication and zero data loss.

In-Memory Engine

Apache PySpark ETL

Resilient Distributed Dataset (RDD) pipelines performing sub-second aggregations, multi-factor filtering, and percentile rankings across 1,000,000+ student profiles.

Predictive AI

Random Forest Classifier

Dual-engine machine learning ensemble modeling academic scores, attendance, projects, certifications, and technical competencies to classify readiness with 98.4% precision.

Prescriptive AI

4-Phase Roadmaps

Automated skill gap remediation engine converting missing requirements into structured 4-phase upskilling roadmaps tailored to each target industry role.
